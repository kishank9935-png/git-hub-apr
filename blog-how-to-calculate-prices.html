How to Calculate Residential Plot Prices in Bhadohi

By APR GROUP OF COMPANY • Published: Real Estate Insights • 4 Min Read

  • 2. Converting Square Feet to Square Yards (Gaj)

Calculating the price of a residential plot in Bhadohi is simple once you understand the basic units of land measurement and how listed per-square-foot rates function. In this guide, we break down the exact mathematics used by real estate professionals.

1. The Basic Square Foot Calculation Formula

In modern plotted real estate developments, prices are quoted as a rate per square foot (Sq. Ft.). The total price is determined by simple multiplication:

Total Plot Price = Plot Area (Sq. Ft.) × Rate Per Square Foot

2. Converting Square Feet to Square Yards (Gaj)

In traditional North Indian terminology, land is often discussed in Gaj (Square Yards). Here is the universal conversion ratio:

  • 1 Square Yard (Gaj) = 9 Square Feet
  • To convert Square Feet to Gaj: Divide by 9. (e.g. 900 Sq. Ft. ÷ 9 = 100 Gaj)
  • To convert Gaj to Square Feet: Multiply by 9. (e.g. 150 Gaj × 9 = 1,350 Sq. Ft.)

3. Calculation Examples for APR Group Projects

Let's calculate the estimated price of a standard 1,000 Sq. Ft. (~111.1 Gaj) plot across our projects:

  • Rajpura Phase 1 (@ ₹2,500/sq.ft.): 1,000 × 2,500 = ₹25,00,000 (₹25 Lakh)
  • Kanshraipur (@ ₹2,300/sq.ft.): 1,000 × 2,300 = ₹23,00,000 (₹23 Lakh)
  • Ugapur (@ ₹1,300/sq.ft.): 1,000 × 1,300 = ₹13,00,000 (₹13 Lakh)
  • Chauri Bazar (@ ₹1,300/sq.ft.): 1,000 × 1,300 = ₹13,00,000 (₹13 Lakh)
  • Parjapatpur (@ ₹1,200/sq.ft.): 1,000 × 1,200 = ₹12,00,000 (₹12 Lakh)
  • Gosai Bazar (@ ₹1,000/sq.ft.): 1,000 × 1,000 = ₹10,00,000 (₹10 Lakh)

4. Reverse Calculation: Finding Land Area from Budget

If you have an exact budget in mind (e.g., ₹15 Lakh), how much plot area can you acquire? Use the reverse formula:

Estimated Land Area = Total Budget ÷ Rate Per Square Foot

At ₹1,000/sq.ft. in Gosai Bazar, ₹15 Lakh yields 1,500 Sq. Ft. (~166.6 Gaj). At ₹2,500/sq.ft. in Rajpura Phase 1, ₹15 Lakh yields 600 Sq. Ft. (~66.6 Gaj).

5. Additional Registration & Documentation Considerations

In addition to the basic land price, buyers should budget for standard statutory registration expenses in Uttar Pradesh, including government stamp duty, registration fees, and deed drafting charges.
