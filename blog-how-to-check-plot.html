How to Check a Plot Before Buying: A Step-by-Step Guide

By APR GROUP OF COMPANY • Published: Real Estate Insights • 5 Min Read

Purchasing a residential plot is one of the most critical financial investments for any family in Bhadohi. While looking at brochures and rate sheets is a good starting point, nothing replaces a comprehensive, hands-on physical site inspection.

At APR GROUP OF COMPANY, we always encourage buyers to walk the ground, measure the dimensions, and assess the location personally. Here is our essential practical checklist for inspecting any residential plot before making a commitment.

1. Inspect Physical Boundaries and Corner Stones

The first step during any site visit is to locate the exact boundary pillars or marking stones of the plot. Never rely solely on an approximate point or visual estimation.

  • Ensure the plot has distinct demarcation showing exact front, back, left, and right limits.
  • Verify whether the plot shape is regular (rectangular or square). Regular shapes maximize usable carpet area when designing a house.
  • Check that there is no encroachment from neighboring plots or agriculture fields.

2. Check Approach Road and Direct Access

A plot is only as valuable as the road leading up to it. When visiting the site:

  • Check the main approach route from the arterial road (e.g. from Rajpura Chauraha or Gyanpur Road).
  • Observe whether the road is paved, gravel-topped, or earthen, and assess vehicular access for construction trucks and four-wheelers.
  • Confirm whether the plot directly touches the sector road and that no private strip lies in between.

3. Ground Level, Soil Quality & Drainage

Waterlogging during monsoon season can significantly inflate foundation and filling costs. During your visit:

  • Compare the plot elevation with the adjacent road. Plots that sit too far below the road level require extensive earth-filling (mitti-patav).
  • Look for natural slope and stormwater runoff pathways.
  • Check the soil firmness to ensure sturdy foundation stability for multi-story residential construction.

4. Orientation and Directional Sunlight

Plot orientation affects ventilation, natural light, and cultural preferences (such as Vastu / directional entry). Identify where the road faces (East, North, West, or South) to envision where main gates, living areas, and windows will sit.

5. Surrounding Neighborhood & Settlement Activity

Look at the nearby vicinity: Are residential homes already inhabited nearby? How close are local daily markets, medical shops, schools, and transport junctions? Projects like Rajpura Phase 1 and Kanshraipur have active surroundings, while locations like Gosai Bazar offer expansive open land for future development.

6. Verify Pricing and Developer Transparency

Finally, always ensure that the per-square-foot rate quoted is transparent and listed openly. At APR GROUP OF COMPANY, all our project rates (ranging from ₹1,000/sq.ft. to ₹2,500/sq.ft.) are published transparently so you can calculate your budget accurately with zero ambiguity.
