Plot Size vs Budget: Complete Residential Land Guide

By APR GROUP OF COMPANY • Published: Real Estate Insights • 5 Min Read

  • 2. Budget Tier 1: Under ₹15 Lakh

One of the most common dilemmas faced by homebuyers in Bhadohi is balancing the desire for a spacious plot with the practical reality of a defined investment budget. Should you choose a smaller plot in a premium location, or a larger parcel in an emerging area?

1. The Golden Formula: Size × Rate = Total Cost

The total plot cost is calculated directly using a transparent mathematical formula:

Total Plot Price = Plot Size (in Sq. Ft.) × Rate per Sq. Ft.

Conversely, if your budget is fixed, the land area you can purchase is calculated as:

Estimated Land Area = Total Budget ÷ Rate per Sq. Ft.

2. Budget Tier 1: Under ₹15 Lakh

For buyers with a budget up to ₹15 Lakh, you have multiple compelling options across APR Group's locations:

  • Gosai Bazar (₹1,000/sq.ft.): You can purchase a generous 1,000 to 1,500 sq. ft. plot (₹10L to ₹15L).
  • Parjapatpur (₹1,200/sq.ft.): Up to 1,200 sq. ft. (~₹14.4 Lakh).
  • Ugapur or Chauri Bazar (₹1,300/sq.ft.): Up to 1,000 sq. ft. (₹13 Lakh).
  • Rajpura Phase 1 (₹2,500/sq.ft.): A compact 500 to 600 sq. ft. starter plot (₹12.5L to ₹15L).

3. Budget Tier 2: ₹15 Lakh to ₹30 Lakh

This is the most popular residential budget bracket in Bhadohi. In this tier:

  • Kanshraipur (₹2,300/sq.ft.): Acquire a 1,000 sq. ft. plot for ₹23 Lakh or 1,200 sq. ft. for ₹27.6 Lakh.
  • Rajpura Phase 1 (₹2,500/sq.ft.): 800 sq. ft. (₹20 Lakh) to 1,000 sq. ft. (₹25 Lakh) in our prime office hub.
  • Ugapur / Chauri Bazar (₹1,300/sq.ft.): Large 1,500 to 2,000 sq. ft. plots for luxury independent homes.

4. Budget Tier 3: Above ₹30 Lakh

Budgets above ₹30 Lakh allow purchasers to secure landmark 1,500 sq. ft. to 2,500+ sq. ft. parcels in Rajpura Phase 1 or Kanshraipur, allowing expansive architectural layouts with private parking and landscaped yards.

5. House Footprint Expectations by Plot Size

  • 600 to 800 Sq. Ft.: Ideal for compact 2BHK duplex residences.
  • 1,000 to 1,200 Sq. Ft.: The classic Indian family footprint accommodating 3BHK to 4BHK with internal parking.
  • 1,500+ Sq. Ft.: Spacious independent villas with open setback areas and front garden.
